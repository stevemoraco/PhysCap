# 27_Talent_Velocity_Recommendations.md
## The Day 100 Hiring Sprint: Actionable Playbook for World-Class Executive Team Assembly

**Document Purpose:** This document provides a detailed, day-by-day execution playbook for hiring 4 C-suite executives (COO, CAO, CRO, CFO) in 100 days. It translates the risks (Document 1) and case studies (Document 2) into concrete actions, decisions, and milestones.

**Context:** The Tavakiev Solar business plan requires a complete executive team by Day 100 to execute brownfield activation (Giga-Foundry 1), greenfield planning (Peak Innovation Park), and digital twin development in parallel. This document is the operational manual for achieving that goal.

---

## Executive Summary: The 100-Day Sprint Structure

### The Strategic Framework

**Guiding Principle:** "Hire for the next 18 months, upgrade for the next 5 years."

Tavakiev does not need "perfect" executives who will lead the company for a decade. It needs "A-minus" executives who can:
1. Execute the brownfield activation at Giga-Foundry 1 (Months 0-12)
2. Secure anchor offtake agreements (Months 0-6)
3. Close Series A financing (Months 12-18)
4. Architect §45X credit monetization (Months 0-12)
5. Build digital twin and humanoid pilot (Months 0-24)

If these executives perform exceptionally, great—offer retention packages and build long-term team. If they underperform, evaluate at Month 12 and upgrade as needed.

**This is not a "hire and pray" strategy. It is a "hire, measure, iterate" strategy.**

### The Four-Phase Timeline

**Phase 1: Foundation (Days 0-30) — "Build the Pipeline"**
- Engage search firms, build candidate database, conduct exploratory calls
- Objective: Vetted "Top 5" list per role (20 candidates total) with confirmed interest

**Phase 2: Evaluation (Days 30-60) — "Separate Signal from Noise"**
- Take-home assignments, first-round interviews, technical deep-dives
- Objective: "Top 2" finalists per role (8 candidates) invited to Assessment Center

**Phase 3: Validation (Days 60-80) — "Work Before Hire"**
- 2-day Assessment Center, 30-day paid consulting sprints
- Objective: Final selection based on real work product quality

**Phase 4: Closing (Days 80-100) — "Lock It In"**
- Formal offers, negotiation, legal agreements, onboarding
- Objective: 4 signed offer letters, start dates confirmed

### Success Criteria

**Minimum Viable Success (Day 100):**
- 3 of 4 executives hired (75% success rate)
- COO and CFO mandatory (operations and capital are non-negotiable)
- CRO or CAO can be hired in Days 100-150 with fractional backup

**Target Success (Day 100):**
- 4 of 4 executives hired (100% success rate)
- All start dates confirmed by Day 120 (allowing for notice periods)

**Exceptional Success (Day 80):**
- 4 of 4 executives hired 20 days ahead of schedule
- At least 1 executive starts by Day 90 (before others give notice)

  4. Top 5 technical risks and mitigation strategies
  5. Budget estimate (Capex and Opex for Months 0-12)
- **Deliverable:** 10-15 page document + 1-page executive summary
- **Evaluation Criteria:**
  - Realistic timeline (not overly optimistic)
  - Evidence of manufacturing discipline (Gantt charts, contingency buffers)
  - Supply chain sophistication (dual sourcing, inventory buffering)
  - Budget realism (no magical cost savings)

**CAO Assignment: "Digital Twin Architecture Design"**
- **Prompt:** "You are the CAO of Tavakiev Solar. Your mandate is to build a digital twin of the entire Giga-Foundry 1 facility (705,000 sq ft, 2 GW capacity) to enable virtual commissioning and AI training for the humanoid workforce. Design the digital twin architecture covering:
  1. Software stack (NVIDIA Omniverse vs. alternatives, why?)
  2. Level of fidelity (high-res physics simulation vs. simplified model, trade-offs?)
  3. Data integration (how to connect real factory sensors/PLCs to digital twin?)
  4. Humanoid training strategy (sim-to-real transfer approach)
  5. KPI gates (what metrics determine if digital twin is 'good enough' to proceed?)
- **Deliverable:** 10-15 page document + architecture diagram
- **Evaluation Criteria:**
  - Depth of technical knowledge (software and robotics)
  - Pragmatism (not over-engineering, focus on "good enough for Phase 1")
  - Clear communication (can explain complex concepts simply)
  - Risk awareness (understands sim-to-real gap challenges)

**CRO Assignment: "2 GW Offtake Strategy"**
- **Prompt:** "You are the CRO of Tavakiev Solar. Your mandate is to secure 2-2.5 GW of bankable offtake agreements within 6 months to underwrite the Series A fundraise. Develop a go-to-market strategy covering:
  1. Target customer list (hyperscalers, EPCs, developers) with prioritization rationale

  4. LOI term sheet template (delivery schedule, payment terms, performance guarantees)

  - Network leverage (references specific contacts at target customers)

**CFO Assignment: "§45X Credit Monetization Model"**

  3. Cash flow timing (when do credits hit bank account?)
  4. Credit buyer landscape (who pays best price for transferred credits?)

- **Evaluation Criteria:**
  - Technical accuracy (credit calculations correct)
