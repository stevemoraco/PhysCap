## Section 2: The Colorado Rapid Response Team - Tactical Permitting Acceleration

### 2.1 How the Rapid Response Team Works

**Official Description:**

The City of Colorado Springs **Rapid Response Team (RRT)** is a program managed by the Economic Development Department to provide qualifying primary employers with **expedited development plan review and permitting.**

**Key Features:**

**1. Timeline Reduction**

RRT reduces, **by about half**, the time it generally takes for plans to move through the approval process.

**2. Priority Processing**

RRT plans are **"literally moved to the top of each agency's priority list"**, reviewed, and forwarded to the next reviewer.

**3. Coordinated Review**

If the company qualifies, City Business Development **coordinates a RRT meeting** with representatives from:
- City Planning
- Building Department
- Fire Department
- Utilities (Colorado Springs Utilities)
- Public Works
- Stormwater Enterprise
- Any other relevant agencies

**4. Concurrent (Not Sequential) Review**

Traditional permitting is **sequential**: Planning → Building → Fire → Utilities (one after another).

RRT enables **concurrent review**: All agencies review simultaneously, dramatically compressing timeline.

**Eligibility Criteria:**

To qualify for RRT, companies must be:
- **Primary employers** (manufacturing, exporting products outside Colorado Springs)
- **Job creators** (bringing significant new employment)
- **Capital investors** (making substantial facility/equipment investments)

**Tavakiev Solar clearly qualifies:** 350 jobs, $500M investment, manufacturing for national market.

**Tavakiev Solar Strategy: Amend Existing Permit (Not New Permit)**

**Permit Amendment Timeline:**

| Permit Type | Typical Timeline | Notes |
|-------------|------------------|-------|
| **New Title V Permit** | 9-12 months | Requires full application, public comment, EPA review |
| **Significant Modification** | 6-9 months | Major process changes, triggers public comment |
| **Minor Modification** | 3-6 months | Smaller changes, limited review |
| **Administrative Amendment** | 1-3 months | Clerical/administrative changes only |

**Tavakiev Solar's Permit Needs:**

**Phase 1 (Module Assembly Only):**

If Tavakiev Solar initially operates **module assembly only** (no cell manufacturing), our air emissions are **minimal:**

**This likely qualifies for Administrative Amendment or Minor Modification** (1-3 months timeline).

**This requires Significant Modification** (6-9 months timeline).

**Economic Multiplier Effect:**

- **1 manufacturing job = 2-3 indirect jobs** (suppliers, services, retail)
- **350 direct jobs = 700-1,050 indirect jobs = 1,050-1,400 total jobs**

**Local politicians cite total job impact** (not just direct jobs) when promoting projects.
