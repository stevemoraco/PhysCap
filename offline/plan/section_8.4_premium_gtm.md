**8.4.2 Why This Isn't a Commodity Market (For Us)**

Traditional solar module pricing has commoditized at $0.12-0.18/W spot because Chinese manufacturers with low-cost labor, subsidized polysilicon, and export VAT rebates compete on price alone. We deliberately avoid this death spiral:

- **Our Net Premium: $0.04-0.08/W, justified by zero-risk domestic supply**

This premium positioning is sustainable because we target customers who:
1. Are legally required to buy domestic (federal/DOD)
2. Need domestic content for ITC optimization (hyperscalers)

These segments represent 40-50% of U.S. solar demand but 60-70% of margins.

**8.4.8 Contract Structures: From LOI to FSA**

We employ phased commitment structures that balance customer risk tolerance with our need for bankable commitments.

**Phase 1: Letter of Intent (LOI)** - Non-binding except exclusivity and confidentiality
- Purpose: Signal mutual interest, reserve allocation, begin technical due diligence
- Volume: Indicative ranges (500 MW - 2 GW over 3-5 years)
- Pricing: Indicative ranges ($0.38-0.42/W, indexed after Year 1)
- Timeline: 60-90 day exclusivity window for CPA negotiation
- Customer value: Option to secure capacity with zero commitment
- Tavakiev value: Demonstrates pipeline to investors, provides negotiation exclusivity

**Phase 2: Conditional Purchase Agreement (CPA)** - Binding, subject to conditions precedent
- Purpose: Firm commitment with customer deposit, contingent on factory/certification milestones
- Volume: Firm minimums (e.g., 400 MW minimum, 600 MW maximum over 3 years)
- Pricing: Fixed for Year 1, indexed formula for Years 2-3
- Deposit: 7-10% of Year 1-2 contract value ($10-30M typical), held in escrow
- Refundability: Refundable if Tavakiev fails conditions precedent (factory commissioning, certifications); non-refundable if customer cancels
- Conditions precedent (Tavakiev): Factory operational by Sept 2026, UL/IEC certs by Oct 2026, PVEL enrollment by Aug 2026
- Conditions precedent (Customer): Credit rating BBB-/Baa3 or higher, or letter of credit; project pipeline confirmation
- Timeline: CPA converts to FSA upon Tavakiev meeting milestones (Month 12-18)
- Customer value: Risk protection (refundable deposit if we fail); priority allocation if we succeed
- Tavakiev value: Bankable commitment (deposit funds working capital); customer economically locked in (forfeits deposit if cancels)

**Phase 3: Framework Supply Agreement (FSA)** - Binding, take-or-pay
- Purpose: Long-term offtake with take-or-pay provisions providing revenue certainty
- Volume: Firm annual minimums (e.g., 500 MW/year minimum, 750 MW/year maximum)
- Pricing: Fixed Year 1, escalator tied to CPI + 1% or weighted commodity index
- Deposit: Applied from CPA deposit; additional 5-10% of Years 3-5 if expanding contract
- Take-or-pay: Customer pays 50% of annual commitment if fails to take delivery (protects our revenue)
- Delivery SLAs: Penalty-backed delivery commitments ($0.02/W per week delay, capped at 15% of shipment value)
- Warranty: 12-year product, 25-year performance (0.5%/year degradation cap)
- Assignability: Customer may assign to affiliates or project SPVs (with consent)
- Term: 3-5 years initial, with 2-year extension option
- Termination: Customer may terminate with 180-day notice, subject to 20-30% termination fee
- Customer value: Long-term price certainty, guaranteed allocation, penalty protection if we miss delivery
- Tavakiev value: Bankable cash flows for project financing, locked-in revenue, protection from customer cancellation
