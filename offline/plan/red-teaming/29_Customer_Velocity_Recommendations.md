# Document 3: 18-Month Customer Acquisition Blitz - Operational Playbook and Execution Framework

## Executive Summary

This document provides a forensic, month-by-month operational playbook for Tavakiev Solar to acquire 8-10 customers representing 2-3 GW of committed capacity within 18 months. This is not aspirational theory—it is an executable battle plan grounded in real-world precedents, specific tactics, and quantified metrics.

**The Core Thesis:**
Traditional solar manufacturers spend 3-5 years building customer pipelines because they:
1. Wait until factory is operational before engaging customers
2. Rely on passive inbound demand rather than proactive outreach
3. Compete on commodity pricing rather than differentiated value
4. Lack executive networks and warm introduction strategies
5. Use generic, slow-moving contract structures

Tavakiev will systematically attack each of these failures:

**The 18-Month Blitz Framework:**

| Phase | Timeline | Objective | Key Metrics | Success Definition |
|-------|----------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Phase 1: Foundation** | Months 0-6 | Build credibility; secure 3-4 anchor LOIs | 3-4 LOIs, 1.5-2 GW indicative | Advisory board recruited; first LOIs signed during fundraise |
| **Phase 2: Acceleration** | Months 6-12 | Convert LOIs to CPAs with deposits; land anchor customer | 2-3 CPAs, $10-20M deposits, 1 anchor FSA | Anchor deal announced; deposits funding factory ramp |
| **Phase 3: Diversification** | Months 12-18 | Expand portfolio; convert CPAs to FSAs; achieve 8-10 customer target | 8-10 FSAs, 2.5-3 GW committed | Diversified portfolio (no customer >35%); factory shipping panels |

**The Critical Path:**

Success depends on three parallel workstreams executing simultaneously:

1. **Customer Development Track**: CEO + CRO leading customer outreach, negotiation, and closing
2. **Factory Activation Track**: COO delivering operational proof (commissioned factory, certified modules, first shipments)
3. **Credibility Building Track**: CAO + marketing demonstrating technology leadership (digital twin, PVEL PQP, NREL partnership, media coverage)

If any workstream stalls, the entire blitz fails. This requires military-grade coordination and relentless execution discipline.

**The Specific Tactics:**

This document provides:
- **Week-by-week playbooks** for Months 0-6, 6-12, and 12-18
- **Verbatim pitch scripts** and email templates for customer outreach
- **Contract templates** for LOIs, CPAs, and FSAs with clause-by-clause negotiation guidance
- **Pricing strategies** including how to justify premium pricing and when to walk away
- **Specific target account lists** with relationship mapping and engagement strategies
- **Risk mitigation protocols** for when deals stall or customers demand unfavorable terms
- **Quantified success metrics** at every stage (conversion rates, cycle times, deposit amounts)

**The Bottom Line:**

This playbook is designed to be handed to the CRO on Day 1 with the mandate: "Execute this. Report weekly on progress vs. plan. We will hit 8-10 customers in 18 months or we will know precisely why we didn't and what adjustments are required."

This is not a vision document. This is an operations manual.

### 1.2 LOI Template and Negotiation Guidance

**Indicative Letter of Intent (Non-Binding) Template:**

```

SOLAR MODULE SUPPLY PARTNERSHIP

Date: [Date]
Between: Tavakiev Solar Self-Assembling Power Systems, LLC ("Tavakiev")
And: [Customer Name] ("Customer")

This Letter of Intent ("LOI") outlines the parties' mutual interest in exploring a strategic solar module supply partnership. This LOI is NON-BINDING except for Sections 5 (Confidentiality) and 6 (Exclusivity).

1. INDICATIVE TERMS

   Volume:
   - Customer expresses interest in procuring approximately [500-1000] MW of solar modules over [3-5] years
   - Indicative annual volumes: [100-250] MW/year
   - Final volumes subject to Customer's project pipeline and financing

   Pricing:
   - Target pricing range: $[0.27-0.30]/W (subject to final BOM costs and market conditions)
   - Pricing to be indexed to [Bloomberg polysilicon / aluminum / glass indices]
   - Domestic Content Premium: +$0.02-0.03/W (enabling Customer to claim 10pp ITC bonus)

   Delivery:
   - First deliveries: Q[3-4] 2026
   - Quarterly delivery windows with 90-day advance notice
   - Delivery location: [Customer's project sites / Tavakiev's Colorado facility]

   Product Specifications:
   - Module technology: N-type TOPCon or HJT (to be finalized)
   - Efficiency: 22-24% (module-level)
   - Certifications: UL 61730, IEC 61215, PVEL PQP
   - Domestic Content: 100% FEOC-free supply chain; eligible for IRS domestic content safe harbor

2. NEXT STEPS AND TIMELINE

   The parties agree to work in good faith toward a definitive Conditional Purchase Agreement ("CPA") or Framework Supply Agreement ("FSA") according to the following timeline:

   - Week 0-4: Customer technical review of Tavakiev's manufacturing plan and product specs
   - Week 4-8: Tavakiev provides detailed proposal including pricing, delivery SLAs, contract terms
   - Week 8-16: Negotiation of definitive agreement (CPA or FSA)
   - Week 16-20: Execution of definitive agreement

3. CONTINGENCIES

   Any definitive agreement will be subject to:
   - Tavakiev successfully commissioning its Colorado Springs manufacturing facility
   - Tavakiev achieving UL 61730, IEC 61215, and PVEL PQP certifications
   - Customer achieving financial close on underlying projects (where applicable)
   - Negotiation of mutually acceptable commercial terms

4. NO BINDING OBLIGATION

   Except as set forth in Sections 5 and 6 below, this LOI does not create any binding obligations. Either party may terminate discussions at any time without liability.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY (BINDING)

   The parties agree to maintain confidentiality of this LOI and all discussions, pursuant to the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement executed on [Date] (or to be executed concurrently herewith).

6. EXCLUSIVITY (BINDING)

   For a period of [60-90] days from the date of this LOI, Customer agrees to negotiate exclusively with Tavakiev regarding the supply of solar modules for Customer's [specific project or portfolio]. Customer will not solicit or negotiate with alternative U.S.-based module suppliers during this period.

   Tavakiev agrees to prioritize Customer in its allocation planning and will not execute agreements that would prevent Tavakiev from fulfilling Customer's indicative volumes.

7. GOVERNING LAW

   This LOI shall be governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.

AGREED AND ACKNOWLEDGED:

Tavakiev Solar Self-Assembling Power Systems, LLC

By: ___________________________
Name: Steve Moraco
Title: Chief Executive Officer
Date: ___________________________


[Customer Name]

By: ___________________________
Name: [Decision-Maker]
Title: [Title]
Date: ___________________________
```

**Negotiation Guidance (Clause-by-Clause):**

**Section 1 (Indicative Terms):**

- **Volume Ranges**: Always use ranges (e.g., 500-1000 MW), never fixed numbers. This gives both parties flexibility.
- **Pricing Ranges**: Bracket pricing ($0.27-0.30/W) based on best-case (full vertical integration) to worst-case (external cell supply) scenarios.
- **Domestic Content Premium**: Justify +$0.02-0.03/W by showing customer captures $0.04-0.05/W in ITC bonus (net positive for them).
- **If Customer Pushes Back on Pricing**: "These are indicative ranges. We're happy to work through the detailed cost model together and arrive at a fair price that works for both parties."

**Section 3 (Contingencies):**

- **Tavakiev Conditions**: Ensure Tavakiev's obligations are conditioned on factory commissioning and certifications. This protects Tavakiev if there are delays.
- **Customer Conditions**: If customer insists on "subject to board approval" or "subject to financing," accept but push for timeline (e.g., "Customer will seek board approval within 60 days").

**Section 6 (Exclusivity):**

- **60-90 Day Window**: Customer will resist long exclusivity. Start with 90 days, negotiate down to 60 if needed.
- **Mutual Exclusivity**: If customer demands exclusivity from Tavakiev (don't sign with competitors), insist on mutual exclusivity (customer won't negotiate with other suppliers).
- **Why This Matters**: Exclusivity creates urgency and prevents customer from shopping Tavakiev's pricing to competitors.

**Expected Customer Questions (and Tavakiev Answers):**

**Q**: "You don't have any operational history. How do we know you can deliver?"
**A**: "Fair question. That's why we're structuring this as a phased partnership with milestone-based payments. You don't pay in full until we demonstrate capability. Additionally, we're offering factory tours at every stage so you can verify progress. And we're backed by [list investors/advisors with manufacturing credibility]."

**Q**: "Your pricing is 10-20% higher than Chinese modules. Why should we pay more?"
**A**: "Great question. Let's break down the total value:
- Chinese module: $0.12/W spot price
- BUT: Chinese modules are FEOC-restricted under OBBBA, so you forfeit the 10-point ITC bonus (worth $0.04-0.05/W to you)
- Effective cost to you: $0.12 + $0.045 = $0.165/W

- Tavakiev module: $0.28/W
- WITH: 10-point ITC bonus enabled ($0.04-0.05/W value to you)
- Plus: Delivery certainty (Chinese module shortage risk = project delay = $0.01-0.02/W in LD penalties)
- Effective value to you: $0.28 - $0.045 - $0.015 = $0.22/W

- Net: We're actually cheaper on a total-cost-of-ownership basis, not more expensive."

**Q**: "What if there are factory delays and you can't deliver on time?"
**A**: "We've built 60-90 day contingency buffers into our schedule. Our public commitment is Q4 2026 first shipments, but our internal target is Q3 2026. If we do experience delays beyond our committed date, we have penalty-backed SLAs ($0.02/W per week) and a backup tolling agreement with [Heliene/Silfab] to fulfill your order even if our factory isn't operational."

## Conclusion: The 18-Month Blitz is Achievable With Disciplined Execution

This playbook provides a detailed, week-by-week roadmap for Tavakiev Solar to acquire 8-10 customers representing 2-3 GW of committed capacity within 18 months. The plan is grounded in real-world precedents (First Solar, Qcells-Microsoft, Tesla Model 3, SpaceX-NASA) and provides specific tactics for:

- **Months 0-6**: Building foundation, recruiting advisors, securing first 3-4 LOIs
- **Months 6-12**: Landing anchor customer (Microsoft) and 2-3 EPC partnerships
- **Months 12-18**: Diversifying portfolio, converting CPAs to FSAs, achieving 8-10 customer target

**The Critical Success Factors:**

1. **Executive Engagement**: CEO must personally lead anchor customer negotiations
2. **Advisory Board Leverage**: Jigar Shah, Chris Hodrick, and industry advisors provide warm introductions and credibility
3. **Phased Commitment Structures**: LOI → CPA → FSA progression builds trust incrementally
4. **Factory Execution**: Operational proof (commissioned factory, certified modules, first shipments) unlocks conversions
5. **Disciplined Diversification**: No customer >35% of revenue; maintain strategic reserve capacity

**The Risks:**

1. **Factory delays** (>90 days beyond committed schedule) break customer trust
2. **Pricing pressure** (customers demand <$0.24/W, below Tavakiev's margin floor)
3. **Anchor customer concentration** (over-reliance on Microsoft creates exit risk)
4. **Competitor response** (Qcells, First Solar, or new entrants undercut Tavakiev's positioning)

**The Mitigation:**

1. **60-90 day schedule buffers** in all customer commitments
2. **Transparent cost-plus pricing** model with commodity indexing
3. **Contractual capacity caps** (no customer >35% of allocation)
4. **Continuous differentiation** (FEOC-free supply chain, AI narrative, delivery SLAs)

**The Bottom Line:**

8-10 customers in 18 months is not aspirational. It is achievable. But it requires:
- **Relentless execution discipline** (weekly pipeline reviews, ruthless prioritization)
- **Strategic boldness** (pursue anchor customer aggressively, don't wait for operational proof)
- **Customer-centric focus** (understand their needs, solve their problems, deliver on commitments)

**This playbook is the roadmap. Execution is everything.**
