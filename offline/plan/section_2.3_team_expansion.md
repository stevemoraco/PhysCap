## 2.3.4 "Hire Late, Fire Fast" Protocol

**Context:** Per 41_Master_Execution_Model.md Principle #9, Tavakiev will NOT follow industry standard hiring patterns. Traditional solar factories hire 200-350 employees for 2 GW capacity. Tavakiev targets <60 Year 1 employees through extreme automation.

### Year 1 Headcount Strategy (Revised from FinalPlan.md Assumptions)

**Previous Plan Implication:** ~350 jobs at full operation (referenced in economic impact claims)

**Revised Plan (Principle #9 Compliant):**

| Phase | Headcount | Timing | Roles | Justification |
|-------|-----------|--------|-------|---------------|
| **Seed (Month 0)** | 8-12 | Day 1-30 | CEO, COO, CAO, CRO, CFO + 3-5 core engineers | Critical path only, no "nice to have" hires |
| **Equipment Procurement** | 20-25 | Month 1-3 | +10-15 engineers (automation, process, electrical) | Hire as equipment orders placed, not before |
| **Commissioning** | 40-50 | Month 4-6 | +20-25 technicians/operators | Only after equipment arrives on-site |
| **Production Ramp** | 50-60 | Month 7-12 | +10 additional operators/engineers | As production scales to 1 GW run-rate |
| **Full 2 GW Operations** | 60-75 | Year 2+ | Minimal additions, mostly robotics engineers | 95% labor elimination via humanoids/automation |

**Total Year 1 Target: 40-60 employees** (vs. industry standard 200-350)

**Total Year 1 Payroll: $15-20M** (vs. industry standard $40-60M)

**Labor Savings: $25-40M/year** (reinvested in automation R&D and Phase 2 expansion)

### 90-Day Review Gates for ALL Hires

**Mechanism:** Every hire (including C-suite) has explicit, measurable KPIs reviewed at Day 90. Termination if <80% achievement.

**Example KPIs (from 27_Talent_Velocity_Recommendations.md):**

| Role | 90-Day KPI | Measurement | Consequence if <80% |
|------|------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **COO** | Equipment installation plan finalized + supplier contracts signed | Gantt chart with critical path + 3 signed MOUs | Immediate termination, activate backup search |
| **CAO** | Digital twin operational + 3 robot task libraries complete | Functional simulation running + documented task success rates | Termination, pivot to conventional automation lead |
| **CRO** | 2 LOIs with anchor customers (500+ MW each) | Signed letters of intent with pricing terms | Termination, activate fractional CRO backup |
| **CFO** | §45X credit monetization strategy + transfer counterparty identified | Financial model + term sheet negotiations initiated | Termination, elevate finance director to interim CFO |
| **VP Policy** | Congressional champion meetings + SEIA leadership role secured | Meeting confirmations + committee appointment | Termination, rely on external counsel (Brownstein Hyatt) |
| **VP Supply Chain** | 3 strategic vendor partnerships locked (Hemlock/NSG/Richardson) | Signed MOUs or supply agreements | Termination, COO absorbs supply chain temporarily |

**Ruthless Execution Standard:** "Bad hires compound (they hire more bad people, create bad culture). Fire fast = minimize damage." - Principle #9

**Cultural Messaging:** "We hire the best, pay unfairly well, and expect exceptional results. If you're not delivering, we'll know by Day 90, and we'll move on. No hard feelings, but no second chances on critical path roles."

### Pay Inequality (Intentional Strategy)

**Rationale:** 10x engineer produces 10x output. Paying them 2-3x median salary is a bargain.

**Pay Bands (Transparent to Team):**

| Performance Tier | Salary Multiple vs. Market | Equity Multiple | Quarterly Review Frequency |
|------------------|----------------------------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| **Top 10%** | 2-3x market | 2-3x standard grant | Annual (retention focused) |
| **Middle 70%** | 1.0-1.5x market | 1x standard grant | Semi-annual |
| **Bottom 20%** | 1x market | 0.5x standard grant | Quarterly (improvement plan or exit) |

**Examples:**

- **Dr. Dennis Hong (CAO, Top 10%):** $700K vs. $400K market = 1.75x + 1.5% equity vs. 0.5% standard = 3x equity
- **Senior Engineer (Top 10%):** $250K vs. $150K market = 1.67x + 0.5% equity vs. 0.1% standard = 5x equity
- **Mid-level Engineer (Middle 70%):** $170K vs. $150K market = 1.13x + 0.1% equity
- **Junior Engineer (Bottom 20%, on watch):** $150K = market + 0.05% equity, quarterly KPI reviews

**Transparency Protocol:** All employees see their performance tier and corresponding compensation rationale. Fairness = performance-based, not role-based.
