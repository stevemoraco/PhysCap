# The 60-Day Fundraise Blitz: Tavakiev Solar's Rapid Capital Deployment Plan

## Executive Summary

**The Challenge: Speed as Existential Requirement**

Tavakiev Solar faces a capital velocity imperative that differs fundamentally from typical venture-backed startups. We are not raising capital to "grow faster." We are raising capital to **secure a time-sensitive, non-replicable asset before competitors** and to **capture a closing market window** driven by hyperscale energy procurement cycles and IRA policy implementation.

**Three Hard Deadlines:**

1. **Asset Acquisition Window (90-120 days):** Babacomari Solar North LLC holds the Meyer Burger HJT equipment via $10.2M credit bid. As a solar developer (not manufacturer), they have 3-6 month economic patience before liquidating to equipment brokers. Competing bids from established manufacturers (Qcells, First Solar, Hanwha) are likely. **Deadline: Q1 2026 (February-March).**

2. **Hyperscale Procurement Cycle (Q1-Q2 2026):** Microsoft, Google, Amazon are conducting vendor qualification and offtake negotiations NOW for 2026-2028 delivery. Framework agreements being signed Q1-Q2 2026. Late arrivals (Q3 2026+) compete for scraps. **Deadline: Qualified vendor status by March 2026, initial offtake agreements by June 2026.**

3. **IRA Credit Optimization (Every Month Matters):** §45X direct pay window (§6417) is 5 consecutive tax years from first production. Beginning production in Q3 2026 vs. Q2 2027 represents 9-12 months of additional credit monetization = $100-200M in time-value. **Deadline: First panel production by September 2026.**

**These deadlines are incompatible with a 6-12 month fundraising cycle.**

This document presents a **60-day capital deployment plan** designed to:

- **Close $150M in capital within 60 days** (anchor investor + debt + rolling VC closes)
- **Deploy first $50M within 72 hours of Close #1** (secure Babacomari assets immediately)
- **Achieve operational milestones during fundraise** (don't wait for "full round" to execute)

The plan is structured in four phases:

**Phase 0: Pre-Launch Preparation (Weeks -8 to 0)** — Build data room, establish relationships, prepare legal infrastructure

**Phase 1: Anchor Investor Sprint (Weeks 0-3)** — Close $50M strategic anchor + $90M debt commitments

**Phase 2: Rolling VC Closes (Weeks 3-6)** — Deploy anchor capital while closing $40M climate VC allocation

**Phase 3: Final Close & Full Deployment (Weeks 6-8)** — Complete raise, deploy all capital to operations

**Total Timeline: 56-60 days from launch to $150M+ fully deployed**

This is not theoretical. This is the playbook used by Cruise (GM acquisition in 45 days), Rivian (Amazon $700M in 45 days), and Databricks ($10B in 60 days). The mechanisms are proven. The question is execution discipline.

   - Prevents founders from selling shares without offering to investors first
   - Timeline: 2-3 days to draft

   - Creates Series Seed Preferred Stock class

   - Timeline: 5-7 days to draft + Delaware filing (3-5 days)
